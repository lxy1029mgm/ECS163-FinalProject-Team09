The Sixth Mass Extinction

The Silent Departure

Extinction is often reduced to a statistic—a line on a graph or a percentage in a report. But every data point represents a silent departure from the canvas of our world.

In 1970, the Spoon-billed Sandpiper was a common sight across Asia's mudflats. Today, it is a ghost. With only ~400 individuals remaining, this tiny traveler has witnessed the world change until there was no place left to land.

This is not just the story of one bird. It is a chronicle of a planet in transition. We invite you to explore the data behind the loss—from the acceleration of time to the roots of human impact.

Our Methodology

Our narrative is built upon the IUCN Red List, the world's most comprehensive inventory of the global conservation status of biological species. We filtered over 159,000 records to isolate 350+ animal species with confirmed extinction status and geographical data. This data reveals not just a loss of life, but a breakdown of an interconnected living system.
